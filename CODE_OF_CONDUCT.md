# Forkluck Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Forkluck community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback with empathy.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes cause harm.
- Keep discussions focused on what is best for the project and its users.
- Protect private kitchen, customer, employee, and account data.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is officially representing Forkluck in public or private spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [guero@forkluck.com](mailto:guero@forkluck.com?subject=Forkluck%20Code%20of%20Conduct). Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or communications and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Anyone enforcing this policy must avoid conflicts of interest and handle reports proportionately, considering context, severity, intent, impact, and patterns of behavior. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
